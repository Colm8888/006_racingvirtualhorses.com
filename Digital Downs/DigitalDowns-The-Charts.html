I am reluctant to post the charts for a number of reasons, however, this site is primarily aimed at the new player/ new stable and it is vital that all stables, especially those starting out know the likelihood of their horse being raceable/competitive.

My reluctance to publish the charts, is that it sets a bar. And horses not reaching the numbers set out in the charts, are then considered NOT to be raceable. This site does not want to discourage stables from running their horses, however on balance it is believed that it is better that stables have an indication of the horses ability and do not get put off by racing very poor horses and in turn leaving or not engaging fully.

The other very important point to make is this .... These charts only give an indication of a horses ability, the charts are based on training times. Training times can be closely matched with a horses race times but can also be misleading. There are many instances of a horse running poor training times and running much improved race times. Only by racing your horse will this be known.

Note: There are only 3 ranges. A horse at the lower end of the "Good" range is very close to being "Very Good". A horse at the higher of the "Good" range is very close to being classed "Average". While both hosres are catogorised in the "Good" range, one might be considered "Very Good" and the other considered "Average".
