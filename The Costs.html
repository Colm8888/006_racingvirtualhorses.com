The Cost of playing

Return SOK to Home Page ...


Site fees are based on a subscription type model. There are various levels of subscription. Once the subscription is paid, there is no cost to nominating your horse to run in a race. See right, the higher the subscription, the more horses you can have in your stable and the more races you can run. OK! Not strictly true, at a guess you can run in 80% of the races for no entry cost, the higher value races may require a supplementary fee to nominate your horse to run.The subscription is a montly fee and can be changed up or down at the end of each month, if you wanted to increase or decrease the number of horses in your stable.


  • Beginner - $14.99

  • Basic - $24.99

  • Stablehand - $34.99

  • Advanced - $49.99

  • Expert - $74.99

  • Professional - $99.99

  • Epic - $149.99

  • Legendary - $199.99

  • Ultimate - $299.99

While it is strictly optional to subscribe to "Premium Features", I don't think many stables would play the game without this information. Premium features give access to a number of features, without which, it is really quite difficult to run your stable or properly assess your horses.

  • 1 Month Premium Access - $10

  • 3 Month Premium Access - $30

  • 6 Month Premium Access - $50

  • 12 Month Premium Access - $95

Practically all other costs are related to the buying of horses, either from other stables, at the auction, from claiming races or from the sales ring. The fourth option to acquire horses, is to breed a yearling, however, you need to have the Sire(s) and Mare(s) to do this.There is also the Item Shop, where you can buy tokens to particpate in higer stakes racing.
