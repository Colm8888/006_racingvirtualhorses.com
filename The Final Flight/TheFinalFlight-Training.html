Training Method

This method will determine all your horses preference on day 1 with the 12 available training runs or at least give a fair indication.

Step One.

Add Shoes and do a PW at 2 miles.
Go to chart and note the distance(s) where your horses stamina falls between 10% and 20%.
For example, if your horse has a stamina of 21% at 2 miles, then I would train at 2 3/4 miles where the horse's stamina falls at 12%.

For you nerds
When evaluating the averages of multiple training runs, the ultimate is to have the range to be greater than 0.5. This way you get closer to the true average and the result are reliably accurate.
For example, say the horse is programmed to run, with just shoes between 2.10.00 and 2.10.60 (A range of 0.6) then the true average will be 2.10.30.
You do a run and get a time of 2.10.10 and another run of 02 is 2.10.20. The range is small 0.1. The average is 2.10.15
This is not the true average. We know the true average to be 2.10.30.
If the range is large, close to 0.5, for example, run 1 is 2.10.10 and run 2 is 2.10.60, the average is 2.10.35 which very closely matches the true average of 2.10.30.
