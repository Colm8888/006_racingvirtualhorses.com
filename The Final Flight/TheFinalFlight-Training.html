Training Method

This method will determine all you horses preference on day 1 with 12 available training runs or at least give a fair indication.

Step One.

Add Shoes and do a PW at 2 miles.
Go to charts and note the distance(s) where your horses stamina falls between 10% and 20%.
For example, if your horse has a stamina of 21% at 2 miles, then I would train at 2 3/4 miles where the horse's stamina falls at 12%.
