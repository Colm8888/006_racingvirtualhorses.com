All horses will live untill at least 9 years of age. After this the horse can expire and the odds of expiring increases as the horse ages.
However, the horses ability declines each year after it reaches 7.
Experience % will increase in line with the more racing a horse does. The higher the grade of race will enhnce this growth.
A horse can retire at any age.
However a horse must be at leat 4 years old before it can breed.
There are 5 grades of horse. S - A - B - C - D.
"S" being the best then "A" and so on. "S" grade horses can only be got from the sales ring. (A - B - C - D from breeding)
My guess is there will be some overlap between grades. E.G. A good "C" may be better than a poor "B".
