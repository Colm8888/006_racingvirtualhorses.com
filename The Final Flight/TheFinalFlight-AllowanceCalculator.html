Allowance Calculator

Determines what weight you horse will carry in an "Allowance" race

  • An allowance race is a race whereby the horses racing weight is determined by its recent race performances.
  • The horse will receive weight allowances based on the number of runs since its last win
  • Or if the horse has never won, the number of races it has previously run.
  • A horse will recice 2lb for each non winning run.
  • The minimum weight a horse can carry is 10St. This means after 7 non-win races the horse does not receive any more allowances.
ALLOWANCE CALCULATOR

Just enter the number of races since the horse last won and click "Calculate"

Note: The Minimum Weight a Horse can carry is 10 St (140 lbs)
