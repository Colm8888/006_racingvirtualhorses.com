The Charts

What Times Dictate a Horse Ability

In time I will put up the charts that indicate horses abilities, what is considered raceable, what is considered top class and all in between.

However, I will not do it now as these charts while useful, especially for the new player can be detrimental.
They often create a divide between horses and stables who then refuse to run horses that do not make this so called grade.

I encourage you to create your own standards, develop you own charts and run what you see fit.
Addittionally, horses can have poor training times but perform to much higher standards on the track.

The Charts

What Times Dictate a Horse Ability

I encourage you to create your own standards, develop you own charts and run what you see fit.
Additionally, horses can have poor training times but perform to much higher standards on the track.
They only way to really know is to run your horse and even that has its limitations.
These charts are crude and do not take into account the horse poulation or current horse standard.
They also do not take into account a horse that is on low maturity or low ground growth.
(Short.cut: Alt + T)

Training times that can be used as indicators of a horse's ability.
These apply to yearling horses only and does not factor in ground growth and maturity.
This chart is best used to evaluate a new yearling.

Use these as a guide only, these are just my opinion.
A horse will very often outrun their paper ability and conversely what looks supreme on paper may not translate to the track.
A Horse doing good/average times may turn out to be excellent after being trained, after maturing and raced.
