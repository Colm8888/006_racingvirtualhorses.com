Horse Traits

All horses will have the following traits.

- Conformation
- Athleticism
- Height
- Balance

Conformation
A horses confirmation is a measure of how well a horse can take racing.
Can be Poor, Average, Good, Excellent or Supreme. All horses can race without penalty after 4 days. If your horses confirmation is supreme you can race without penalty after 3 days.

Athleticism
A horses Athleticism is a measure of how well a horse can jump.
Can be Poor, Average, Good, Excellent or Supreme. If the Althleticism is Supreme, the horse will never fall.

Height
The horses height may help or hinder a horses performace depending on the characteristics of the track on which the horse is running..
The range I have recorded is ....... smallest 14.10, largest 16.00..
Therfore .....
Small is 14.0 to 14.50 - My guess is this size horse is best suited to tight tracks like Ayntree, Kalso and Muscleburgh..
Average is 14.50 to 15.50
Tall is 15.0 to 16.0 - Perhaps suited to galloping tracks like Haydock and Azcot..
These are my assumptions, I do not know that these assumptions are correct.

Balance
Balance is a measure of how well a horse will handle an undulating track like Sejfield, Cheltnham or Xeter.
Can be Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent or Supreme.
