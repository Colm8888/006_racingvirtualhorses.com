Stamina Chart


I have a PW for every horse at 2M. You can compare what your horse does at 2M with the chart below. From that PW you can predict what stamina he will be at other distances.

These are actual values that I have recorded for my horses. The Chart will added to as I PW more and buy more horses.

Use the stamina remaining after a race as a guide only. Historically it was believed that between 10% and 16% was optimal. The final flight site is not as hung up on remaining stamina as other sites. I find 8% can be okay and up 20%.

Concern yourself with times and training stars to gauge your horses preferred distance.

You will often see many duplicate results, this is a measure of how likely that the charts are correct.
