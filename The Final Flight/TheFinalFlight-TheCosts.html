Stable Maintenance £'s Depends on Stable Size

More Information

Ongoing recurring costs can be a drain on resources.

To test your horse fully you will need to apply all the adds. Additionally you will need to replace the adds every 2 months. This can be as much as £2.50 per horse per month. 30 Horses in your stable equates to £75 per month.

Note: this is absolutely the worst case scenario, and is unlikely as not all horses will need all the adds. However for 30 horses £35 or £40 per month to re-apply Shoes, Blinkers, Cheek pieces and Tongue Tie is realistic.

In addition, you will need to renew your premiun subscription at £10 per month. Although this fee is optional it is difficult to play the game properly without it.

The game has been updated for Season 3, wherby you have an option to opt for free adds for the season and forego any bonuses. Alternatively, pay for the adds and receive bonuses. Each stable will need to decide based on their own prefences and stable size.
