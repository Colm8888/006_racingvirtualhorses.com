First off, claiming and claimers is not really a big aspect of the game here on Sport of Kings. There are a few reasons for this.

  • Horses, especially 2YO horses are all at different levels of maturity and there is no way to know this level of maturity, the horse you claim could be on 20% or on 100% (fully mature) for example. This makes it a risk.

  • For 2YO horses the cost of claiming a horse is $15 or $25.
    For older horses (3YO+) the cost of claiming a horse is $10, $15 or $25.
    As these races typically attract horses at the lower end, to pay these prices does not normally represent value for monney.

There will always be exceptions and a top horse may occasionally appear in a claimer if you are able to spot it.
Especially take a look in the higher purse claimers.





Some will use the race betting as a guide in the hope that the shorter priced horse indicates that horses superiority.

However, this betting (for some reason, which I have never seen explained) is only available on mobile devices (Phone and Tablet) and not on a desktop PC.
