There are 2 main ways to accumulate "Bonus Credits" ........... The first way

Race payouts will sometimes be made up of cash and Bonus Credits. In this example above, of the $25 purse, $10 is cash and $15 is bonus credits. Note: this is the total prize money. As first place receives 50% of the purse, the winner will receive $12.50, of which 2/5's ($5) is cash and 3/5's ($7.50) is in bonus credits.

The second way .......

Bonus Credits are paid out based on run and win targets achieved throughout the season

Bonus Credits are also paid out based on run and win targets achieved throughout the lifetime of the stable


  • Bonus Credits can be used for .........

  • Master Trainer

  • Breeding Evaluations

  • Premium Access

  • Stamina Recovery

  • Changing Silks

  • Horse Maturity Evaluations

  • In part to pay for Sales Ring horses

  • Subscriptions
